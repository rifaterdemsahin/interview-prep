    Role play — live interviewer

    Setup
    Pick a role, flip the card, then answer out loud or in writing and press Submit to interviewer. The interviewer will acknowledge your answer, push back with real follow-up probes, then give a verdict you can save.

    How to use this for a London Inside IR35 interview

    1. Work the deck top to bottom in order — recruiter screens first, then IR35 and commercials, then technical and behavioural.

    2. Speak every answer with the mic. Contract interviews in London are screening calls; your spoken fluency is being assessed more than your typing.

    3. Answer in under 90 seconds, hit the numbers (rate, notice, years, users, latency, cost saving), and finish with the outcome.

    4. Inside IR35: never say you will control how you deliver. Frame everything as client-directed delivery through an umbrella company, and confirm you are comfortable working as a deemed employee for tax purposes.
